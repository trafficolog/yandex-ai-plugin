# Optimization decision model

Optimization is a decision under uncertainty, not a collection of hard-coded rules.

For each proposed action capture:

- entity and placement;
- observation window;
- spend/click/conversion volume;
- business target and current result;
- conversion delay / attribution caveat;
- likely cause;
- proposed change and blast radius;
- confidence: high / medium / low;
- rollback condition.

Prefer reversible steps: isolate query intent, adjust negatives, repair tracking/landing mismatch, narrow obvious waste, or change one strategy constraint at a time. Avoid simultaneous structural, budget, and strategy changes that make causality unreadable.
