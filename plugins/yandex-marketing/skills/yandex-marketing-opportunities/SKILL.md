# Marketing opportunities

Use explicit finding classes rather than a magic score: measurement/KPI/attribution risks, demand/query candidates, budget/performance candidates, landing/traffic hypotheses, seasonality alerts, and optional SERP context.

Each finding carries kind, confidence, evidence, limitations and next step. Search may enrich intent but cannot establish paid efficiency. Do not convert an analytical candidate into a live change without delegation and owning-plugin safety checks.
