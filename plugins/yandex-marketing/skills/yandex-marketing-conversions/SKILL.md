# Conversion reconciliation

Always preserve the KPI fingerprint: business objective, goal IDs, attribution context, metric basis, currency, VAT basis, and period when relevant.

Never sum Direct conversions with Metrika conversions, and never sum overlapping Direct/Metrika revenue or cost. Compare them as separate observations. Classify reconciliation as ALIGNED, EXPLAINABLE_DIFFERENCE, REVIEW, or INCOMPARABLE.

A discrepancy is not automatically a tracking defect. Check goal semantics, attribution, period/date basis, maturity, data lag and report scope before forming a hypothesis.
