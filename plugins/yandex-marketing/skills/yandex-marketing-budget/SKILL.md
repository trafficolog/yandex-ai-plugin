# Budget opportunities

Budget analysis requires explicit business KPI context, comparable campaigns, sufficient evidence, and mature outcomes. No universal CPA, ROAS, click-count, or winner/loser threshold is allowed.

Produce a reversible proposal and exact preview with evidence, confidence, limitations, blast radius and review criteria. Any live budget change must be delegated to yandex-direct-budget and requires explicit approval immediately before the owning plugin writes.
