# Demand planning

Wordstat demand is external demand evidence and is not guaranteed ad inventory. A high-count phrase with weak Direct coverage may be a DEMAND_EXPANSION_CANDIDATE, but Wordstat count must never be labeled missed traffic, lost impressions, or guaranteed available volume.

Align region, device, period and phrase context where they materially affect interpretation. Review targeting, negatives, budget, bidding, schedule, strategy and eligibility before proposing paid coverage changes.
