# Content gaps

Wordstat demand alone produces `DISCOVERY_CANDIDATE`, not a confirmed gap. Promote to `CONTENT_GAP` only when Search shows the site absent or materially weak and Webmaster supplies compatible visibility evidence; retain the exact evidence coverage and limitations. A missing page may justify a content recommendation, but this skill never publishes content or executes Webmaster writes. Keep cluster bridge risk and period/region mismatch visible in the finding.
