# Alignment rules

Verified design baseline: 2026-09-01.

Temporal alignment is `EXACT`, `APPROXIMATE`, or `MISMATCHED`. Search SERPs are point-in-time snapshots. Wordstat rolling windows are not silently converted into calendar-month reports. Keep ranking region, query region and visitor geography distinct. `MISMATCHED` evidence may be described side by side but must not support causal or trend claims.
