# Metrika audit framework

Use `PASS / ISSUE / REVIEW / N/A` and cite observed evidence. Do not use universal bounce-rate, time-on-site, conversion-rate or ecommerce thresholds as facts.

## Identity and configuration
- correct counter/site association
- timezone and relevant settings
- access/ownership context when available

## Goals and conversions
- configured goals versus actual business outcomes
- duplicate/obsolete goals
- conversion coverage and naming clarity
- goal data sufficiency

## Ecommerce
- ecommerce data presence
- orders/revenue consistency with available business source
- currency/context

## Acquisition quality
- source/channel mix
- UTM completeness and consistency
- Direct linkage/cost consistency when relevant

## Data quality
- sampling and sample share
- data lag
- sensitive-data disclosure limits
- missing periods or suspicious discontinuities

## Attribution
- model used by the report
- whether the model matches the decision being made

For each issue state evidence, impact, confidence and the smallest reversible next action.
